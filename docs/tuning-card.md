# Tuning Card — `drive_example()` in `snakeoil3_v1.py`

`driver/snakeoil3_v1.py` is the only driver file; each version is a Git tag (see the changelog). Section 1 below describes `v1.32` (the current driver). **Since v1.15 the steering inside the planned-line zone (60–3,330 m, one zone) is the line-follower (`plan_*`); the sensor steering knobs (`line_*`, `apex_*`, `setup_*`, `lookahead_gain`, `rel_*`, `run_*`) act only outside it (the first 60 m and the finish straight from 3,330 m), and every `line_table` / `turn_table` row is inert. Since v1.16 the allowed speed inside `plan_mem` (90–1,750 m and 2,600–2,850 m) is the line's stored speed itself: the sensor-plan knobs (`brake_margin`, `brake_decel`, `brake_aero`, `brake_max*`, `brake_hi_*`, `brake_load_*`, `corner_speed`, `turn_grip*`, `turn_steer_*`, `grip_boost`, `slip_ref`) and the `corner_table` act only outside it (first 90 m, 1,750–2,600 m, 2,850 m to the finish). Since v1.17 the line-error guard (`plan_ek`, `plan_e0`, `plan_emax`) cuts the allowed speed while the car is off the line. Since v1.19 the exit guard (`plan_xk`, `plan_x0`, `plan_xt`) takes throttle off inside `plan_mem` while the car runs outside the planned line, and inside `plan_tz` the throttle follows the stored speed's slope (`plan_tk`, `plan_tla`, `plan_tmax`). Since v1.21 the launch allowance lasts to `launch_v` 160 km/h and the straight exits have their own `exit_v` (130). Since v1.22 the car shifts down on the throttle as soon as the lower gear lands below `drive_ds_rpm` (`drive_ds_wait` steps after the last shift); braking and coasting keep `downshift_rpm`. Since v1.23 the car, braking or coasting inside `entry_ds_zones`, takes the lower gear as soon as it lands below `entry_ds_rpm` (19,000); since v1.31 the same inside `entry_ds2_zones` at `entry_ds2_rpm` (17,000); `downshift_rpm` decides everywhere else. Since v1.25 the 1,931 m bend and since v1.26 the Corkscrew's left apex are on re-drawn stretches of the stored line. Since v1.27 the follower's heading and position gains are scaled down at the Corkscrew's left flick while the car is outside its line (`plan_gz`, `plan_gv0`, `plan_gvw`, `plan_gw`, `plan_khlo`, `plan_kplo`). Since v1.30 the stored speed at 2,280–2,360 m follows the track's elevation (a compression at 2,335–2,358 m before the crest; `tools/elevation.py`).** **Since v1.32 the stored line at 540–1,300 m (770 m and 1,042 m) is drawn wider (0.85–0.95 for 0.65) and driven with less of its own speed.** Patterns on v1.32: 6 reversals, 41 shifts (2 undone within 1 s); top speed 293 km/h; the lap's own maximum `|trackPos|` 0.861 at 1,037 m (v1.31: 0.831, v1.28: 0.795). Margin places at v1.32: 1,037 m 0.926 (follower check), 1,527 m 0.917 (braking-plan check), 767 m 0.884 (gears check), 1,036 m 0.862 (suites). Driver frozen at v1.32 (2026-10-08). `snakeoil3_gym.py` is the untouched original (`target_speed = 300`) and is what `gym_torcs.py` imports.

The client runs at about **50 steps per second** (one step ≈ 21 ms). Every step it reads all sensors, runs `drive_example()`, and sends all actions back.
